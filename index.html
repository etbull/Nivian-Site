About Nivian

Nivian helps Australian businesses adopt AI safely, privately, and with complete confidence.

01

Our Mission

Our mission is to make AI accessible and compliant for all. There are very real security risks with inputting sensitive data to public AI systems. We empower firms to use AI without sacrificing privacy, security, or regulatory requirements.

02

Our Approach

Practical, simple solutions, built around your workflows. We integrate AI directly into your tools and documents so it actually improves daily operations, not complicates them. No over-engineering or complex interfaces.

03

Our Values

Clarity. Security. Humility. Every system we build should be simple and able to be understood by anyone while also maintaining the highest privacy standards. We also keep things grounded and honest, no ego, no jargon.

FAQ

Everything you need to know about our AI solutions

Australian privacy laws, including the Privacy Act 1988, require businesses to protect personal information. Hosting your AI in Australia ensures compliance with these regulations, keeps your data within Australian legal jurisdiction, and protects against foreign access requests. For industries like healthcare and legal services, this is often mandatory.

The AI Assistant plans (Core and Pro) provide ready-to-deploy AI assistants customised to various degrees. Core allows you to have custom branding, system prompts, models, and full Australian data residency. Pro does the same except it has full integration with SharePoint, OneDrive, and document libraries as well as email search and custom data-source integration. We can also fine-tune pro assistants to improve at specific tasks your business needs.

The AI Assistant plans (Core and Pro) provide ready-to-deploy AI assistants with varying levels of integration. Core is perfect for basic AI assistance, while Pro adds document and email search capabilities. Copilot Agents are built from scratch for your specific business problems, offering complete flexibility and tailored solutions for unique requirements.

Nivian Assistants are hosted in your Microsoft Tenancy. We force Australian residency for all resources and enforce this with Azure policy to ensure compliance and peace of mind. We can also host in other regions if this is required. Contact us for more info.

Absolutely. The Pro AI Assistant and Copilot Agents integrate seamlessly with Microsoft 365, including SharePoint, OneDrive, Outlook, and Teams. We configure secure access using your existing authentication systems and permissions, ensuring AI respects your current security policies and user access controls.

The AI Agents can connect multiple data sources together as well as perform custom tasks. We can embed it within your current setup using API's exposed by your current tools.
